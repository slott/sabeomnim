# Sabeomnim (사범님) - Taekwondo Learning App
## Compose Multiplatform Architecture & Curriculum Specification

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Sabeomnim** is a cross-platform mobile application (Android & iOS) built using **Compose Multiplatform (CMP)**. The app serves as a comprehensive study companion for Taekwondo practitioners progressing from **White Belt (10th Geup)** to **1st Dan Black Belt (Il Dan / Cho Dan)** under the **World Taekwondo (WT / Kukkiwon)** syllabus.

The core pillars of the application are:
1. **Curriculum & Grading Guide**: Detailed requirements per belt rank (stances, strikes, blocks, kicks).
2. **Interactive Theory & Quiz System**: Belt-specific Q&A addressing technical terminology, Korean names, philosophy, referee signals, and grading oral exam questions.
3. **Poomsae Video Section**: High-definition video player for Taegeuk 1 through 8 with multiple angles, step-by-step movement breakdown, and cadence audio.
4. **Progress & Grading Readiness Tracker**: Checklist and quiz mastery tracking so students and instructors can evaluate readiness for promotional tests.

## 2. Belt Progression & Curriculum Specification (10th Geup to 1st Dan)

WT / Kukkiwon grading generally follows a 10-Geup system before 1st Dan/Poom. Below is the structured breakdown for each grade.

---

### Grade 10: White Belt (10th Geup - Baek-tti / 백띠)
* **Symbolism**: Signifies innocence, having no previous knowledge of Taekwondo; a blank canvas.
* **Poomsae**: Basic movements (Kibon Dongjak / 기본 동작) or Taegeuk Il Jang foundation.
* **Techniques**:
  * *Stances (Seogi)*: Moa-seogi (Attention stance), Naranhi-seogi (Parallel stance), Ap-seogi (Walking stance), Ap-kubi (Forward stance).
  * *Blocks (Makgi)*: Arae-makgi (Low block), Momtong-makgi (Middle block), Olgul-makgi (High block).
  * *Strikes/Punches*: Momtong baro-jireugi (Straight punch), Bandae-jireugi (Reverse punch).
  * *Kicks (Chagi)*: Ap-chagi (Front snap kick), Bal-deung chagi (Instep kick).
* **Sample Theory Q&A**:
  * **Q**: What does Taekwondo mean?  
    **A**: *Tae* (태) = foot/kicking; *Kwon* (권) = hand/punching; *Do* (도) = the art, path, or way of life.
  * **Q**: What is the meaning of the White Belt?  
    **A**: Innocence and the beginning of the journey, representing a seed waiting to grow.
  * **Q**: How do you say "Attention" and "Bow" in Korean?  
    **A**: *Charyeot* (차렷) and *Gyeong-nye* (경례).
  * **Q**: What are the five tenets of Taekwondo?  
    **A**: Courtesy (Ye-ui), Integrity (Yeom-chi), Perseverance (In-nae), Self-Control (Geuk-gi), Indomitable Spirit (Baek-jeol-bul-gul).

---

### Grade 9: White Belt with Yellow Stripe (9th Geup)
* **Techniques**:
  * *Stances*: Refining Ap-kubi and introducing Dwit-kubi (Back stance) basics.
  * *Blocks*: Momtong an-makgi (Inner middle block), An-palmok momtong-makgi.
  * *Kicks*: Dollyo-chagi (Roundhouse kick) fundamentals, Ap-cha-olligi (Leg front stretch).
* **Sample Theory Q&A**:
  * **Q**: How do you count from 1 to 10 in Korean?  
    **A**: Hana (1), Dul (2), Set (3), Net (4), Daseot (5), Yeoseot (6), Ilgop (7), Yeodeol (8), Ahop (9), Yeol (10).
  * **Q**: What is the Korean word for training hall and uniform?  
    **A**: Training hall is *Dojang* (도장); uniform is *Dobok* (도복).
  * **Q**: What is the Korean word for Master / Instructor?  
    **A**: *Sabeomnim* (사범님).

---

### Grade 8: Yellow Belt (8th Geup - No-rang-tti / 노랑띠)
* **Symbolism**: Signifies the earth from which a plant sprouts and takes root.
* **Poomsae**: **Taegeuk 1 Jang (태극 1장)** — Represents **Keon (건)**, the sky and light; the source of all creation. (18 movements).
* **Techniques**:
  * *Stances*: Ap-seogi, Ap-kubi.
  * *Blocks*: Arae-makgi, Momtong-makgi, Olgul-makgi.
  * *Strikes*: Momtong-jireugi.
  * *Kicks*: Ap-chagi.
* **Sample Theory Q&A**:
  * **Q**: What is the meaning of Taegeuk 1 Jang?  
    **A**: It symbolizes *Keon* (Heaven/Sky), representing the origin of everything and the foundation of life. It should be performed with power and clarity.
  * **Q**: How many movements are in Taegeuk 1 Jang?  
    **A**: 18 movements.
  * **Q**: What target area is *Momtong*?  
    **A**: The torso/middle section (from solar plexus to collarbone).
  * **Q**: Why do we shout (Kihap) in Taekwondo?  
    **A**: To focus energy, tense core muscles on impact, intimidate opponents, and demonstrate confidence.

---

### Grade 7: Yellow Belt with Green Stripe (7th Geup)
* **Poomsae**: **Taegeuk 2 Jang (태극 2장)** — Represents **Tae (태)**, internal firmness and external gentleness (Joyfulness/Lake). (18 movements).
* **Techniques**:
  * *Stances*: Dwit-kubi introduction, Ap-kubi stabilization.
  * *Blocks*: Olgul-makgi combined with punches.
  * *Strikes*: Olgul-jireugi (High punch).
  * *Kicks*: Dollyo-chagi (Roundhouse kick) with proper hip rotation.
* **Sample Theory Q&A**:
  * **Q**: What does Taegeuk 2 Jang symbolize?  
    **A**: *Tae* (Lake), representing inner strength paired with outer calmness and joy.
  * **Q**: What is the difference between *Baro-jireugi* and *Bandae-jireugi*?  
    **A**: *Baro-jireugi* is punch delivered with the rear hand (matching the front foot is Bandae in WT, or reverse punch depending on stance definition). Specifically, *Baro* = punch with the same side hand as the rear leg (reverse punch), while *Bandae* = punch with the same side hand as the forward leg (regular punch).
  * **Q**: What is the target area for *Olgul*?  
    **A**: The head/face area (above the collarbone).

---

### Grade 6: Green Belt (6th Geup - Chor-ok-tti / 초록띠)
* **Symbolism**: Represents the green plant growing vigorously, developing technical skills.
* **Poomsae**: **Taegeuk 3 Jang (태극 3장)** — Represents **Ri (리)**, fire and sun; enthusiasm, heat, and unpredictability. (20 movements).
* **Techniques**:
  * *Blocks*: Hansonnal momtong bakkat-makgi (Single knife-hand middle outer block).
  * *Strikes*: Sonnal mok-chigi (Knife-hand neck strike).
  * *Kicks*: Yeop-chagi (Side kick) basics, continuous Ap-chagi.
* **Sample Theory Q&A**:
  * **Q**: What does Taegeuk 3 Jang symbolize?  
    **A**: *Ri* (Fire/Sun), signifying passion, brightness, and controlled energy.
  * **Q**: What part of the hand is used in *Sonnal*?  
    **A**: The knife edge of the open hand (blade of the hand).
  * **Q**: What part of the foot strikes during an *Ap-chagi* vs a *Yeop-chagi*?  
    **A**: *Ap-chagi* uses the ball of the foot (Ap-chuk); *Yeop-chagi* uses the knife-edge or heel of the foot (Bal-nal / Dwichuk).

---

### Grade 5: Green Belt with Blue Stripe (5th Geup)
* **Poomsae**: **Taegeuk 4 Jang (태극 4장)** — Represents **Jin (진)**, thunder; power and majestic bravery. (20 movements).
* **Techniques**:
  * *Stances*: Dwit-kubi (Back stance) mastered.
  * *Blocks*: Sonnal momtong-makgi (Double knife-hand middle block), Bakkat palmok momtong bakkat-makgi.
  * *Strikes*: Pyeon-son-kkeut jjireugi (Spear hand thrust), Jebipoom mok-chigi (Swallow neck strike).
  * *Kicks*: Yeop-chagi with chamber and pivot.
* **Sample Theory Q&A**:
  * **Q**: What does Taegeuk 4 Jang symbolize?  
    **A**: *Jin* (Thunder), representing dignity, sudden immense power, and calmness in the face of danger.
  * **Q**: In *Dwit-kubi*, how is weight distributed between front and rear feet?  
    **A**: Approximately 70% on the back leg and 30% on the front leg.
  * **Q**: What is *Pyeon-son-kkeut jjireugi*?  
    **A**: Straight spear finger thrust, usually targeting the solar plexus supported by a downward palm block.

---

### Grade 4: Blue Belt (4th Geup - Cheong-tti / 파란띠)
* **Symbolism**: The blue sky towards which the plant is reaching as it matures into a tree.
* **Poomsae**: **Taegeuk 5 Jang (태극 5장)** — Represents **Son (손)**, wind; gentle flexibility yet destructive force. (20 movements).
* **Techniques**:
  * *Stances*: Koa-seogi (Cross stance).
  * *Blocks*: Me-jumeok naeryo-chigi (Hammerfist downward strike), Danggyeo teok-jireugi (Pulling chin punch).
  * *Strikes*: Palkup dolryeo-chigi (Elbow turning strike), Palkup pyojeok-chigi (Elbow target strike).
  * *Kicks*: Naeryeo-chagi (Axe kick / downward kick), consecutive kicks.
* **Sample Theory Q&A**:
  * **Q**: What does Taegeuk 5 Jang symbolize?  
    **A**: *Son* (Wind), symbolizing adaptability, fluidity, and storm-like strength.
  * **Q**: What is *Koa-seogi* and when is it used?  
    **A**: Cross stance; used when transitioning quickly sideways or absorbing momentum after landing.
  * **Q**: What part of the body strikes in *Palkup-chigi*?  
    **A**: The elbow.

---

### Grade 3: Blue Belt with Red Stripe (3rd Geup)
* **Poomsae**: **Taegeuk 6 Jang (태극 6장)** — Represents **Gam (감)**, water; ceaseless flow, flexibility, overcoming obstacles. (19 movements).
* **Techniques**:
  * *Blocks*: Hansonnal olgul bakkat-makgi, Arae hecheo-makgi (Low spreading block), Batangson momtong-makgi (Palm heel middle block).
  * *Kicks*: Dwi-chagi (Back kick / turning back kick), Dollyo-chagi + Dwi-chagi combinations.
* **Sample Theory Q&A**:
  * **Q**: What does Taegeuk 6 Jang symbolize?  
    **A**: *Gam* (Water), continuous flow, adaptability, and conquering obstacles through steady movement.
  * **Q**: What is *Batangson*?  
    **A**: The palm heel of the hand.
  * **Q**: How do you avoid hooking during a *Dwi-chagi*?  
    **A**: Keep the kicking knee brushing closely past the standing knee and kick straight back like a mule, rather than swinging wide.

---

### Grade 2: Red Belt (2nd Geup - Hong-tti / 빨간띠)
* **Symbolism**: Danger. The student has acquired significant technique and power; caution and self-control are vital.
* **Poomsae**: **Taegeuk 7 Jang (태극 7장)** — Represents **Gan (간)**, mountain; steadfastness, immobility, firm foundation. (25 movements).
* **Techniques**:
  * *Stances*: Beom-seogi (Tiger stance), Joochoom-seogi (Horse-riding stance).
  * *Blocks*: Hansonnal arae-makgi, Batangson geodeureo momtong an-makgi, Gawi-makgi (Scissors block), Bo-jumeok (Covered fist).
  * *Strikes*: Deung-jumeok bakkat-chigi (Backfist outer strike), Mureup-chigi (Knee strike), Dujumeok jeocho-jireugi (Double upset punch).
  * *Kicks*: Pyojeok-chagi (Target kick).
* **Sample Theory Q&A**:
  * **Q**: What does Taegeuk 7 Jang symbolize?  
    **A**: *Gan* (Mountain), representing stability, unwavering presence, and knowing when to advance or hold ground.
  * **Q**: Describe *Beom-seogi* (Tiger stance).  
    **A**: Most weight (90%) is on the bent rear leg, front heel raised with only the ball of the foot touching lightly, ready to spring or kick.
  * **Q**: What is *Gawi-makgi*?  
    **A**: Scissors block — simultaneous low block (Arae-makgi) and outer forearm middle block (Momtong bakkat-makgi).

---

### Grade 1: Red Belt with Black Stripe (1st Geup)
* **Symbolism**: The dawn before the black belt; synthesis of all geup ranks and mastery over self.
* **Poomsae**: **Taegeuk 8 Jang (태극 8장)** — Represents **Gon (곤)**, earth; the motherly source of receiving, nurturing, and completion. (27 movements).
* **Techniques**:
  * *Blocks*: Oe-santeul-makgi (Single mountain block), Sonnal hecheo-makgi.
  * *Strikes*: Danggyeo teok-jireugi, Momtong dubeon-jireugi (Double punch).
  * *Kicks*: Du-bal dangsang ap-chagi (Jumping two-step front kick), Dwi-hurigi (Spin hook kick).
* **Sample Theory Q&A**:
  * **Q**: What does Taegeuk 8 Jang symbolize?  
    **A**: *Gon* (Earth), representing full reception, fertility, grounding, and preparation for Dan rank.
  * **Q**: What is the final movement of Taegeuk 8 Jang?  
    **A**: Step forward into Ap-kubi with Momtong Bandae-jireugi and a decisive Kihap.
  * **Q**: How many movements does Taegeuk 8 Jang have?  
    **A**: 27 movements (the longest of all Taegeuk poomsaes).

---

### 1st Dan: Black Belt (Il Dan / 1단 - Cho Dan) & Junior Poom
* **Symbolism**: The opposite of white; mastery of fundamentals, impervious to darkness and fear, beginning of true study.
* **Poomsae**: Preparation for **Koryo (고려)**; mastery of all 8 Taegeuk poomsae.
* **Knowledge Requirements for 1st Dan Exam**:
  * History of World Taekwondo and Kukkiwon (founded 1972).
  * Taekwondo's Olympic inclusion (debuted as demonstration sport in 1988 Seoul, official medal sport in 2000 Sydney).
  * Competition rules (scoring zones, Gam-jeom deductions, referee commands: *Sijak*, *Kalyeo*, *Guman*, *Kye-sok*).
  * Philosophy of the Dobok (white represents purity/unification), Ti (belt represents focus/unity of mind and body), and O-haeng (5 elements of Taegeuk).

## 3. Video Section Architecture: Taegeuk Poomsae Player & Subtitles

### 3.1 Dual-Angle Synchronized Video Playback
Taekwondo poomsae requires spatial awareness in multiple dimensions. The player will feature a seamless dual-angle camera switcher:
* **Camera Angles**:
  * **Angle 1 (Front View - 0°)**: Standard judge/testing perspective. Ideal for alignment, shoulder squareness, and chest level.
  * **Angle 2 (Side/Profile View - 90°)**: Essential for stance length (Ap-seogi vs Ap-kubi vs Dwit-kubi), knee bend depth, and chamber distance.
* **Instant Angle Toggle**:
  * Switching between Angle 1 and Angle 2 preserves the exact millisecond playback position (`currentPositionMs`) and playback state (playing vs paused).
  * Optional *Side-by-Side Split View* or *Miniature Picture-in-Picture (PiP)* preview of the secondary angle on tablet/large screens.

### 3.2 Dynamic Move Subtitles & Technical Overlays
Rather than relying on basic burned-in subtitles, the app utilizes a **structured time-coded metadata engine** (JSON-backed timeline synced with standard WebVTT fallbacks):
* **On-Screen Move HUD (Heads-Up Display)**:
  * **Step Counter**: e.g., `Step 04 / 18`
  * **Korean Script (Hangul)**: e.g., `오른 앞굽이 몸통 반대지르기`
  * **Revised Romanization**: e.g., `Oreun Ap-kubi Momtong Bandae-jireugi`
  * **English Definition**: e.g., `Right Forward Stance, Middle Regular Punch`
  * **Technique Breakdown Badges**: Stance: `Ap-kubi` | Target: `Momtong` | Action: `Jireugi`
  * **Key Form Checkpoints**: Brief coaching tips (e.g., "Lock rear heel down; front knee aligned over ankle").
* **Audio Voice-Over Option**:
  * Optional toggle to hear Korean cadence calls (*Hana! Dul!*) at each move trigger.

### 3.3 Precision Scrubbing & Playback Controls
Martial arts technique analysis requires frame-accurate inspection:
* **Interactive Step Timeline / Scrubber**:
  * Slider marked with visual step notches (dividers for each movement).
  * Dragging snaps smoothly or allows micro-second scrub with timecode readout (`00:14.20`).
* **Step Navigation Buttons**:
  * `[ |< ] Previous Step`: Instantly rewinds to the start of the previous movement.
  * `[ ⟲ ] Loop Step`: Continuous loop mode for the current movement for repetitive drill practice.
  * `[ >| ] Next Step`: Fast-forwards to the start of the subsequent movement.
* **Speed Rate Adjustments**:
  * Granular slow-motion presets: `0.25x`, `0.5x`, `0.75x`, `1.0x`.
* **Gesture Controls**:
  * Double-tap left/right edge: Skip back/forward 2 seconds.
  * Single tap on screen: Toggle playback HUD and pause/resume.
  * Vertical swipe on right side: Volume; vertical swipe on left side: Brightness.

## 4. Korean Terminology Audio Dictionary & Pronunciation Trainer (한국어 용어집)

### 4.1 Overview & Functional Scope
A dedicated audio-visual dictionary enabling students to browse, search, and hear authentic Korean Taekwondo terminology. Each term includes:
* **Korean Hangul** (e.g., 차렷, 앞굽이, 돌려차기)
* **Revised Romanization** with phonetic syllable breakdown (e.g., *Char-yeot*, *Ap-ku-bi*, *Dol-lyo-cha-gi*)
* **English Translation & Dojang Context** (e.g., "Attention stance – feet together, fists clenched at sides")
* **One-Tap Audio Playback**: Instant soundbite with visual waveform/ripple animation when playing.
* **Slow Audio Toggle (0.75x)**: Plays the audio slower so beginners can hear individual Korean syllable enunciations clearly.
* **Belt-Level Filter**: Ability to filter terms relevant to a student's current belt level (e.g. "Show me only White Belt terms").

### 4.2 Terminology Categories
1. **Dojang Commands & Etiquette (도장 구호 및 예절)**:
   * *Charyeot* (차렷 - Attention), *Gyeong-nye* (경례 - Bow), *Junbi* (준비 - Ready stance), *Sijak* (시작 - Start/Begin), *Kalyeo* (갈려 - Break/Pause), *Kye-sok* (계속 - Continue), *Guman* (그만 - Stop), *Shwieo* (쉬어 - Relax/At ease), *Dwiro-dora* (뒤로 돌아 - Turn around).
2. **Numbers & Counting (숫자 세기)**:
   * Native Korean (1-10 for forms/kicks): *Hana, Dul, Set, Net, Daseot, Yeoseot, Ilgop, Yeodeol, Ahop, Yeol*.
   * Sino-Korean (1-10 for Geup/Dan/Poomsae): *Il, Ee, Sam, Sa, O, Yuk, Chil, Pal, Gu, Sip*.
3. **Stances (서기 - Seogi)**:
   * *Charyeot-seogi*, *Naranhi-seogi*, *Joochoom-seogi*, *Ap-seogi*, *Ap-kubi*, *Dwit-kubi*, *Beom-seogi*, *Koa-seogi*, *Hak-dari-seogi*.
4. **Strikes & Punches (지르기 / 치기 / 찌르기)**:
   * *Baro-jireugi*, *Bandae-jireugi*, *Momtong-jireugi*, *Olgul-jireugi*, *Sonnal mok-chigi*, *Deung-jumeok bakkat-chigi*, *Me-jumeok naeryo-chigi*, *Palkup-chigi*, *Pyeon-son-kkeut jjireugi*.
5. **Blocks (막기 - Makgi)**:
   * *Arae-makgi*, *Momtong an-makgi*, *Momtong bakkat-makgi*, *Olgul-makgi*, *Sonnal momtong-makgi*, *Batangson-makgi*, *Gawi-makgi*, *Oe-santeul-makgi*, *Hecheo-makgi*.
6. **Kicks (차기 - Chagi)**:
   * *Ap-chagi*, *Dollyo-chagi*, *Yeop-chagi*, *Naeryeo-chagi*, *Dwi-chagi*, *Bandae-dollyo-chagi / Dwi-hurigi*, *Du-bal dangsang-chagi*, *Pyojeok-chagi*.
7. **Body Anatomy & Target Zones (신체 부위 및 공격 목표)**:
   * *Olgul* (Face/High), *Momtong* (Torso/Middle), *Arae* (Groin/Low), *Jumeok* (Fist), *Sonnal* (Knife hand), *Batangson* (Palm heel), *Ap-chuk* (Ball of the foot), *Bal-nal* (Foot blade), *Dwichuk* (Heel).
8. **Titles & Equipment (호칭 및 용품)**:
   * *Sabeomnim* (Instructor/Master), *Kwanjangnim* (Grandmaster), *Sunbaenim* (Senior student), *Hubae* (Junior), *Dobok* (Uniform), *Ti* (Belt), *Hogu* (Chest protector).

### 4.3 Multiplatform Audio Architecture
The audio engine uses a resilient two-tier strategy:
1. **Primary: Bundled Studio Audio Clips**:
   * Authentic high-fidelity sound clips recorded by a native Korean martial artist, bundled in `composeApp/commonMain/composeResources/files/audio/`.
   * Ensures authentic martial arts cadence and pronunciation (standard TTS often sounds overly robotic and lacks martial arts inflection).
2. **Fallback: Native Text-to-Speech (TTS) Provider**:
   * Android: `android.speech.tts.TextToSpeech` (Locale: `Locale.KOREA`).
   * iOS: `AVSpeechSynthesizer` with `AVSpeechSynthesisVoice(language: "ko-KR")`.
   * Seamlessly falls back to TTS if a custom recorded audio file is not yet bundled.
