### 1. Introduction
Sabeomnim ("we", "our", or "the App") is an educational mobile application designed to help practitioners learn World Taekwondo (WT / Kukkiwon) curriculum, forms (Poomsae), and terminology. 

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

### 2. Zero Personal Data Collection
Sabeomnim does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). 
Specifically:
* **No Account Required**: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address, name, or phone number.
* **No Personal Tracking**: We do not track your location, device identifiers (advertising IDs), or contact lists.
* **No Cookies or Ad Networks**: The app contains zero advertisements and no third-party marketing SDKs.

### 3. Local On-Device Storage
All user preferences (such as selected language, theme preference, speech voice preference, and quiz progress) are stored strictly locally on your physical device using standard platform storage APIs (`SharedPreferences` on Android, `NSUserDefaults` on iOS). This data never leaves your device.

### 4. Audio & Text-to-Speech
Audio pronunciations utilize either pre-bundled local audio assets or your device's built-in platform Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine. We do not record your microphone or capture any ambient audio.

### 5. Network Usage
The application operates fully offline. Network connectivity is only utilized when streaming optional high-definition reference video media from public repository servers (e.g., GitHub Releases CDN). No user-identifying telemetry is attached to these media requests.

### 6. Crash Reporting (Optional Diagnostics)
If enabled by the operating system, anonymized crash reports may be sent via standard platform mechanisms to help identify and resolve software bugs. These diagnostic reports contain no personally identifiable information.

### 7. Children's Privacy
Sabeomnim does not address anyone under the age of 13 for data collection because we do not collect data from any user regardless of age. The content is suitable for users of all ages (PEGI 3 / Everyone).

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly in this document and accessible via the Google Play Store listing.
