Privacy Policy for Sabeomnim (사범님)

Last Updated: September 18, 2026
Application ID: dk.slott_hansen.sabeomnim
Contact: msh@slott-hansen.dk

1. Introduction

Sabeomnim ("we", "our", or "the App") is an educational mobile application designed to help practitioners learn World Taekwondo (WT / Kukkiwon) curriculum, forms (Poomsae), and terminology. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Sabeomnim does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details.
  • No Personal Tracking: We do not track your location, device identifiers, or contact lists.
  • No Advertising: The app contains zero advertisements and no third-party marketing SDKs.

3. Local On-Device Storage

All user preferences (language, theme mode, audio voice selection, and quiz progress) are stored strictly locally on your physical device using standard platform storage APIs (SharedPreferences on Android, NSUserDefaults on iOS). This data never leaves your device.

4. Audio & Text-to-Speech

Audio pronunciations utilize either pre-bundled local audio assets or your device's built-in platform Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine. We do not record your microphone or capture ambient audio.

5. Network Usage

The application operates fully offline. Network connectivity is only utilized when streaming optional high-definition reference video media from public repository servers (e.g., GitHub Releases CDN). No user-identifying telemetry is attached to these media requests.

6. Children's Privacy

Sabeomnim does not collect data from any user regardless of age. The content is suitable for all ages (PEGI 3 / Everyone).

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: msh@slott-hansen.dk
