## Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

Master World Taekwondo (WT / Kukkiwon) curriculum from White Belt (10th Geup) to Black Belt (1st Dan) with Sabeomnim (사범님) — your personal Taekwondo instructor and training companion.

Whether you are stepping onto the dojang mat for the very first time or refining your forms for a black belt promotion test, Sabeomnim provides comprehensive, structured instruction directly aligned with Kukkiwon standards.

---

### 🥋 COMPLETE 10 GEUP TO 1ST DAN CURRICULUM
Progress step-by-step through every belt rank with clear requirements, stances, and techniques:
• 10th Geup (White Belt / 백띠) — Basic stances (Moa, Naranhi, Ap-seogi, Ap-kubi), Low block (Arae-makgi), Middle punch (Momtong-jireugi), Front kick (Ap-chagi).
• 9th Geup (White/Yellow Stripe) — Momtong an-makgi, Dollyo-chagi basics.
• 8th Geup (Yellow Belt / 노랑띠) — Taegeuk 1 Jang (태극 1장).
• 7th Geup (Yellow/Green Stripe) — Taegeuk 2 Jang (태극 2장), Dwit-kubi stance.
• 6th Geup (Green Belt / 초록띠) — Taegeuk 3 Jang (태극 3장), Sonnal mok-chigi, Yeop-chagi.
• 5th Geup (Green/Blue Stripe) — Taegeuk 4 Jang (태극 4장), Sonnal momtong-makgi.
• 4th Geup (Blue Belt / 파란띠) — Taegeuk 5 Jang (태극 5장), Palkup-chigi, Me-jumeok.
• 3rd Geup (Blue/Red Stripe) — Taegeuk 6 Jang (태극 6장), Batangson-makgi, Dwi-chagi.
• 2nd Geup (Red Belt / 빨간띠) — Taegeuk 7 Jang (태극 7장), Beom-seogi, Gawi-makgi.
• 1st Geup (Red/Black Stripe) — Taegeuk 8 Jang (태극 8장), Oe-santeul-makgi, Dwi-hurigi.
• 1st Dan (Black Belt / 1단 Cho Dan) — Koryo preparation, Kukkiwon philosophy, Olympic WT competition rules, and Dan grading theory.
