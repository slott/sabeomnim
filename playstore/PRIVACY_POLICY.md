## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Sabeomnim** ("the App"), developed by Morten Slott Hansen ("we", "us", or "our"). Sabeomnim is a martial arts learning companion designed for World Taekwondo (WT / Kukkiwon) practitioners.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information when you download, install, and use Sabeomnim on Android and iOS devices.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Sabeomnim is built with a strict **privacy-first** approach:

* **No User Accounts or Registration**: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details (such as your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth) to use the App.
* **No Personal Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).
* **No Financial or Payment Information**: The application does not process payments or collect billing information.
* **No Sensitive Device Sensors**: We do not access your device's camera, microphone, GPS/location, contacts, photo library, or biometric hardware.
* **No Advertising Identifiers or Cross-App Tracking**: We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or tracking frameworks (such as IDFA or Google Advertising ID).

---

## 3. Information Collected Automatically (Diagnostics & Stability)

To ensure the reliability, performance, and stability of the application, Sabeomnim uses **Firebase Crashlytics** (provided by Google LLC).

When the App experiences an unexpected crash or freeze, anonymous technical diagnostic information is automatically sent to Google Firebase servers:

* **Crash Diagnostics**: Stack traces, application state at the time of the crash, and internal exception details.
* **Device Metadata**: Device model, operating system version (e.g., Android 15), device orientation, and available system RAM/storage at the time of the event.
* **Installation Identifiers**: Randomly generated, non-personal Firebase installation identifiers used solely to aggregate error counts.

> **Note**: Crash reports do not contain personal information, martial arts training notes, or any data capable of identifying you individually. Google processes this information in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase Crashlytics Terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/crashlytics).

---

## 4. Local Data Storage on Your Device

All training and preference data is stored **locally on your device** and never transmitted to external servers:

* **App Preferences**: Your chosen interface language (English or Danish), visual theme (Dark Mode, Light Mode, or System Default), and audio voice gender preference (Male or Female).
* **Curriculum & Quiz Progress**: Completed belt requirements, bookmarks, and quiz assessment scores.

This data remains exclusively on your device. Uninstalling the application will permanently remove all locally stored preferences and progress.

---

## 5. Media & Network Access

* **Poomsae Video Streaming**: The application streams instructional multi-angle Taegeuk Poomsae video files hosted via secure content delivery networks (GitHub Releases CDN). When streaming videos, standard network requests are made containing IP addresses necessary for internet data delivery, subject to standard HTTP protocols.
* **Audio Pronunciation**: Spoken Korean terminology pronunciation is generated locally using bundled pre-recorded audio or your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine (`android.speech.tts.TextToSpeech` on Android, `AVSpeechSynthesizer` on iOS). No spoken voice audio is recorded, transmitted, or uploaded.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Sabeomnim is designed for practitioners of all ages, including children studying Taekwondo. 

* We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union).
* Because the App collects no personal information, it complies fully with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and Article 8 of the General Data Protection Regulation (**GDPR**).
* If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take appropriate measures.

---

## 7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not collect personal data on external servers, there is no personal data to retain or delete. You can delete all local data at any time by clearing the application's cache and data through your device settings, or by uninstalling the application.
